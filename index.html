Who We Are

A campaign built on service, not slogans.

The Accord Party's Oyo State ticket is grounded in one idea: government should show up where people actually live — in the wards, the markets, the clinics, and the classrooms.

🎖

Our Mission

To deliver accountable, people-first governance across all 33 local government areas of Oyo State.

👁

Our Vision

An Oyo State where every ward has functioning schools, clinics, roads, and real economic opportunity.

⚖️

Core Values

Integrity, transparency, inclusion, and consistent presence in the communities we hope to serve.

🤝

Community Service

Regular town halls, ward-level outreach, youth clinics, and skills workshops ahead of 2027.

The Ticket

Meet the candidates.

Two names, one accord — running together for Oyo State in 2027.

01

Olaleye Kammel Olatunji (MASCOT)

Federal House of Representatives

Ibadan North Federal Constituency · 2027

Olaleye Kammel Olatunji (MASCOT) has spent over a decade organizing at the grassroots level across Ibadan North, working directly with market associations, youth groups, and community elders to identify practical solutions to everyday problems.

Experience

A background in public policy advocacy and constituency-level project coordination, with hands-on experience translating community concerns into legislative priorities.

Vision

A federal constituency where representation means visible, measurable delivery — not just attendance in Abuja.

Community Impact

Led youth empowerment drives and supported small-business owners with market infrastructure advocacy across Ibadan North wards.

02

Oriyomi Hamzat Abdul Rahmon

Governor, Oyo State

Accord Party · 2027

Oriyomi Hamzat Abdul Rahmon brings a career spanning public administration and private-sector enterprise to the Accord Party's governorship bid, with a focus on institution-building over personality politics.

Leadership Experience

Years of leadership across community development initiatives and organizational management, with an emphasis on structured, accountable delivery.

Vision

A modern, well-run Oyo State where public institutions work for citizens first, and prosperity is shared across every senatorial district.

Community Achievements

Championed education and healthcare access initiatives and mentorship programs for young professionals and entrepreneurs across the state.

The Plan

Ten pillars for Oyo State.

Our manifesto is organized around the priorities Oyo State residents raise most often at ward meetings.

01

Education

Rebuilding public schools and expanding scholarship access.

02

Healthcare

Functional primary healthcare centers in every ward.

03

Youth Empowerment

Skills acquisition hubs and start-up support for young people.

04

Agriculture

Modernizing farm access roads and produce storage.

05

Technology

Digital literacy programs and public-service digitization.

06

Infrastructure

Sustained investment in roads, drainage, and public utilities.

07

Security

Community policing partnerships and rapid-response coordination.

08

Economic Development

Support for small businesses, markets, and local industry.

09

Women Empowerment

Access to capital, healthcare, and leadership opportunities.

10

Employment

Public-private partnerships to create sustainable local jobs.
